Cellsea

an eyeless thing in an endless sea

You are a handful of hexagonal cells drifting in an expanse with no edges. Build a body at the bench, then go take cells off of everything else that lives out here.

Hearts keep you alive — lose them all and you dissolve. Normal cells are your muscle; strip them and you crawl. Armour is tough but heavy. Spikes shear off whatever cell they touch, and clack harmlessly off other spikes. Flagella push away from themselves — mount them at the back to go forward.

W or click to beat your flagella · A / D or the mouse to turn · E or the BENCH button opens the bench anywhere, any time.
